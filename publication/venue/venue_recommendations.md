# Venue recommendations

These are fit recommendations, not verified deadline announcements. Current
deadlines and formatting requirements must be checked on each official venue
page before submission.

## Realistic conference

**IEEE Conference on Games (CoG).** The topic fit is strong for game
analytics, esports telemetry, and applied game AI. The paper should foreground
the evaluation protocol and honest uncertainty rather than claim a new
algorithm. It needs the missing holdout and stronger external validation before
being presented as a mature result.

## Alternative conference

**Foundations of Digital Games (FDG).** The work fits data-driven game
research and telemetry analysis. The contribution would need a clear
game-analytics research question and a complete reproducibility statement.

## Realistic journal

**IEEE Transactions on Games.** The scope is appropriate, but the current
study is not yet journal-ready: prospective/chronological validation,
fifth-sample verification, and more complete protocol coverage are needed.

No claim is made here about current page limits, review model, or deadlines;
those details are time-sensitive and were not locally verified from official
calls for papers.
