# Contributions

1. An auditable grouped CS2 round-outcome benchmark with saved identifiers,
   predictions, thresholds, and validation-only selection.
2. A time/coverage analysis that reports both natural cohorts and matched
   test rounds rather than treating late-round scores as directly comparable.
3. A conservative comparison of event, state, geometry, occupancy, and
   compact temporal representations, with cluster-aware uncertainty.

These are methodological and empirical contributions. They are not claims of
causal feature value, prospective generalization, or a new canonical STOM
implementation.
