# Limitations

- The test partition was previously inspected, so results are exploratory.
- The split is grouped by match but not chronological or team-held-out.
- The fifth-sample holdout is absent and cannot support a conclusion.
- Only 5--75 second cutoffs were executed; 90--120 seconds are unavailable.
- Cached snapshot provenance does not fully support independent raw-tick
  re-extraction.
- The original combined tick feature cache is missing.
- Planar Euclidean distances omit walls, levels, visibility, and navigation.
- The number of independent matches limits inference for small strata.
- Historical STOM notebooks have unverified external data/split provenance.
