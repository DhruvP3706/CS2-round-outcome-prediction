## Transferable ideas

William Spearman's **Beyond Expected Goals** (MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference, 2018; [author/employer PDF](https://static.hudl.com/craft/downloads/SSAC2018_Beyond_Expected_Goals.pdf)) motivates using continuous spatial context in football. Its title in the old bibliography was expanded beyond the actual title. Pitch influence is a modeling analogy, not proof that Euclidean CS2 proximity measures tactical control. Walls, levels and line of sight make a direct transplant unreliable.

Shi et al., **Convolutional LSTM Network: A Machine Learning Approach for Precipitation Nowcasting**, NeurIPS 2015 ([primary proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2015/hash/07563a3fe3bbe7e3ba84431ad9d055af-Abstract.html)), introduces convolutional input/state transitions for spatial-temporal forecasting. Occupancy sequences could use this approach, but sparse players, maps of different geometry and small numbers of independent series differ from dense meteorological fields. Our experiment tests compact causal sequences; it does not claim to implement ConvLSTM.

STOM is used here as the repository's spatial-temporal occupancy representation, not as a universally standardized method with an invented citation. The new contribution is exact geometry, observed occupancy and auditable paired evaluation. The literature suggests richer context and careful prediction horizons; it does not justify a guaranteed 0.80 target.
